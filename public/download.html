Signing In & Account Roles

Section 1

👑 Owner

  • Full access to all features
  • Manage staff, products, wallet
  • View all reports and history
  • Billing and plan management

🧑‍💼 Cashier

  • Take orders at the register
  • Cash and credit sales
  • View own wallet balance
  • No access to owner settings

📋 Manager

  • Record bar expenses
  • View items and customers
  • Stock check access
  • No billing or staff management
1

Open the app

Launch P.O.S. Pro from your browser or the installed APK.

2

Enter your username and password

Owners register first. Cashiers and managers are created by the owner inside the app.

3

You'll land on your role's home screen

Owners and cashiers go to the Bar register. Managers go to Items.

💡

Forgot your password? Use the Forgot Password link on the login page to reset via email.

Opening & Closing the Bar Session

Section 2
1

Open the Bar tab

Tap Bar in the menu. If no session is open you'll see the Open Session button.

2

Start selling

Once open, cashiers can take orders immediately. All sales are logged in real time.

3

Close the session (owner)

At end of day the owner closes the session from the Wallet page. This locks the session totals.

🍺

Bar (POS) — Taking Orders

Section 3
1

Tap items to add to cart

Browse by category tabs at the top. Tap any product to add one unit to the cart.

2

Adjust quantities

Long-press an item in the cart to change its quantity or remove it.

3

Choose cash or credit

Tap Cash Sale or Credit Sale to proceed to checkout.

💡

Sort items by tapping the sort icon (⇅) at the top right of the product grid.

💵

Cash Sales & Change

Section 4
1

Tap Cash Sale

After building the order, tap the Cash Sale button at the bottom of the cart.

2

Enter amount tendered

Type in the amount the customer gives you. The app calculates change instantly.

3

Confirm the sale

Tap Confirm. The sale is recorded and the cashier wallet is updated.

💳

Credit Sales (Charge to Tab)

Section 5
1

Tap Credit Sale

Select Credit Sale from the cart. You'll be prompted to choose a customer.

2

Select or create a customer

Search existing credit accounts or tap + New Account to create one on the spot.

3

Confirm charge

The amount is added to that customer's outstanding balance.

💡

Customers pay off their tab from the Customers section. Tap a customer then Add Payment.

📦

Items (Product Catalog)

Section 6
1

Tap Items in the menu

Only owners and managers can access this section.

2

Add or edit a product

Set name, category, selling price, cost price, and stock level. Optionally add a photo.

3

Partial Offline Mode & Sync

Section 13

P.O.S. Pro supports partial offline operation. If your connection drops after logging in, you can continue taking sales — but only from the Register page.

⚠️

Partial offline mode. If your internet connection is lost after login, stay on the Register (Bar) page to keep taking sales. Sales made while offline are queued locally and will auto-sync once your connection is restored. Navigating away from the Register page or closing the app while offline may cause unsaved sales to be lost.

💡

All other features — wallets, reports, staff management, and settings — require an active internet connection. Use a stable Wi-Fi or mobile data connection at your bar for the best experience.

💡

Install the app as a PWA (Add to Home Screen in your browser) for a native-app feel and faster load times.
